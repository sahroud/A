SAHROUD TASBIH - Offline Mukabbir Audio

Required audio asset:
  tasbih/audio/takbir.mp3

The file should contain a clear, single male Arabic recitation of:
  اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

Recommended format:
  MP3, mono or stereo, 44.1 kHz, 96-192 kbps.
  Keep the beginning clean (no long silence). A short natural room/reverb tail is acceptable.

The application now loads this local file instead of relying on Android speechSynthesis.
Until the real audio file is added at the exact path above, the app intentionally reports that the audio asset is missing. No generated tone or synthetic placeholder is presented as a human voice.

Additional prayer-stage audio files can be added after the corresponding prayer-stage transitions are implemented and mapped to the user's settings.
